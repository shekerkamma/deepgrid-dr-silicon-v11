dark variant recoloured to the site palette (Page `#0b1220`, edges `#8fa3bf`, one accent: Light Blue `#7fb0e6`).

These are DeepGrid's own product plans, not public facts, so the skill's web-search check does not apply; the
Blueprint is the source of record. No "first" or comparative claims. The closing frame carries the site's
mandatory caveat: pre-silicon, concept illustration.
