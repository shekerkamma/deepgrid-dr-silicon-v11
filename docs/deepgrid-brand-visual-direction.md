## Source selection

179 unique large images were recovered from four named DeepGrid decks and the supplied DeepGrid source archive; 75 non-slide candidates were visually inventoried. Many text-bearing deck images contain older throughput, funding or commercial claims and were not imported into the website. Selected fleet and driver-assistance artwork comes from the client-ready DeepGrid commercial-fleet GTM deck. It is labelled as illustrative application context, never customer or deployment evidence.

Original source hashes, deck members, generation specifications, reviewed status and dimensions are recorded in `deepgrid-brand-image-provenance.json`. Images are encoded as full-frame WebP at native/768px widths with embedded origin/specification metadata. The initial DG32 concept was corrected to remove protruding leads; only the corrected flat-package concept ships. Visual concepts establish no silicon measurements or qualification.
