The feeling curve moves from orientation to comparison, peaks at the fault-path demonstration, and resolves in an explicit evaluation step. Retain the existing mix of editorial flow, portfolio selection, functional visual stages and qualification registers. Do not impose a pinned film or repeated entrance on reading sections.
