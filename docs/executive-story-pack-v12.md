# DeepGrid v12 — executive story pack

## 1. BLUF
DeepGrid should earn a customer’s next commitment by connecting a specific equipment problem to a proposed product benefit, then showing the evidence and adoption work still required.

## 2. Audience decision
A business leader, programme owner or product evaluator should be able to decide which opportunity to investigate, why it matters, and what must be established before investment or adoption. Engineering detail supports that decision; it does not determine the reading order.

## 3. Tension
The previous site led with implementation: clocks, blocks, source excerpts and retrieval traces. Readers had to construct the business meaning themselves. A citation panel was incorrectly treated as an executive storyboard. This rebuild changes the argument and the page body, not merely the label.

## 4. Argument arc
1. Name the customer problem and the business decision.
2. Explain why the existing system or sourcing approach creates a need.
3. State the proposed product contribution in plain language.
4. Show the specific evidence that supports evaluation today.
5. Explain the trade-off, dependencies and limits of that evidence.
6. Close with a proportionate next action or decision gate.

### Component contract
- Navigation and search: orient the reader by product, business task and decision; no implementation explanation.
- Hero: state the customer-facing proposition and its scope before internal architecture.
- Product overview: explain the replacement or system role, with current maturity alongside it.
- Feature cards: present the proposed value, the customer implication and adoption gate. Detailed block diagrams remain supporting evidence.
- Architecture and technical sections: lead with the decision a constraint affects. Exact specifications remain accessible in a disclosure; do not turn a design target into a delivered benefit.
- Applications: start from the operating or service problem, then identify the role of each part.
- Evidence: explain which commitment a result can support and what it cannot establish.
- Company and team: connect strategy and responsibilities to a specific programme. Recognition does not establish product readiness.
- Resources and media: help the reader select the evidence needed; an animation explains a mechanism and does not prove performance.
- Contact and closing links: leave a concrete next decision, not simply a file list.
- Ask: answer → business implication → adoption gate → next decision. Supporting sources are optional disclosure. Retrieval scores, seeds, paths, developer labels and raw catalog specifications are absent from the executive response.

### Worked storyboard: “How is D100 funded?”
1. **D100 follows mature-node revenue; it has a separate funding plan.** Lead with the proposed ₹50 Cr round and exclusion from the FY31 ₹1,000 Cr plan. Direct evidence: Blueprint p. 43, “Where it sits in the plan”.
2. **The investment follows the mature-node business.** Explain the sequencing implication. Fair synthesis of the same passage.
3. **Development cost and qualification determine the delivery case.** Evidence: p. 43, “What it earns”; p. 44, “Status”.
4. **Review D100 as a separate investment decision.** Agree trigger, milestones and qualification plan. Evaluation guidance, not an assertion of secured funding or investment advice.
5. **Sources and supporting detail.** Exact cited passages are available after the business argument, without retrieval mechanics.

## 6. Evidence map
Direct evidence: Blueprint product scope, replacement, status and D100 funding plan; existing architecture records and corrective claims. Fair synthesis: editorial product summaries and comparisons, which retain product boundaries and pre-production qualifications. Interpretation: business implications and recommended next decisions, expressed as evaluation guidance. No new customer contracts, ROI, qualification, manufacturing result or guaranteed benefit is asserted. Technical source documents are not rewritten.

## 7. Content cuts
Remove retrieval traces, graph scores, raw excerpt dumps and technical specification cards from the main Ask response. Cut implementation-first opening copy and guaranteed effectiveness language. Keep exact technical parameters, diagram labels and evidence limits in the supporting layer. Do not remove inconvenient maturity information or manufacture a commercial claim to sound executive.
